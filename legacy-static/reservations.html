Reservation FAQs

Are walk-ins welcome without a reservation?

Yes! Our Zen Terrarium, main espresso bar, and reading corners always retain 40% open seating for spontaneous walk-ins. However, the private ASMR Cocoon Pods and Therapy Cat sessions fill up quickly on weekends, so reservations are highly advised.

What if I am running late?

We hold tables and pods for 20 minutes with zero penalties. Simply reply to your SMS confirmation text if you need an extra 15-minute grace period!

Can I work on my laptop in the ASMR Pods?

Silent typing on laptops is completely allowed! However, all video calls or audio playback without headphones are strictly prohibited inside the pods and reading sanctuary to protect the collective tranquility.
