The Origin Story

We Loved Coffee. We Hated the Modern Anxiety That Came With It.

Modern coffee culture had lost its soul. Ear-splitting espresso steam wands, harsh fluorescent LEDs, blaring pop music, and high-strung rush hours turned what was supposed to be a daily pause into an engine of anxiety. In 2023, we set out to change that forever.

The Turning Point

Born in Tokyo, Refined by Neuroscience

Our co-founder, Dr. Maya Sterling (a neurochemist researching chronic sympathetic activation) and Julian Vance (an acoustic architect) met at an overcrowded cafe in Tokyo. Both were suffering from severe cognitive burnout.

Looking around, they noticed a striking paradox: every patron was holding a cup of liquid caffeine while visibly trembling from screen fatigue, tight neck muscles, and sensory overload.

"What if a cafe was engineered in reverse?" they asked. "What if every single sensory input—the light wavelength, the acoustic decibel, the mineral balance of the water, the adaptogens in the espresso—was calculated to soothe the amygdala and stimulate the vagus nerve?"
