Sensory Accommodations & Visiting FAQs

We take sensory accommodations seriously. We offer complimentary sanitized loop earplugs, Bose noise-dampening headphones, dimming eye shades, and 15-lb weighted lap blankets upon request. Furthermore, our ASMR Cocoon Pods feature completely adjustable lumen and color temperature dials so you control your lighting environment 100%.
Well-behaved dogs on leashes are warmly welcome on our outdoor garden patio at both locations. Certified service animals are permitted inside all indoor zones with the exception of the enclosed Paws & Purrs Therapy Cat Lounge, which is kept isolated to protect our resident therapy cats and guests with severe pet dander allergies.
Silent remote work and typing are encouraged in our Silent Reading Sanctuary and general lounge! We offer gigabit fiber Wi-Fi. However, to preserve collective serenity, speaking out loud on Zoom or cell phone calls is strictly restricted to our outdoor terrace or designated phone booths.
